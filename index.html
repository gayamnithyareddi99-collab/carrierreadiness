FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CarrierReadiness and what it means for your logistics firm's operational efficiency in the active readiness era.

What is CarrierReadiness and why does my logistics firm need it?

+
CarrierReadiness (CORP) is an AI-powered platform that assesses, validates, and accelerates carrier onboarding ensuring compliance, reducing risk, improving data accuracy, and enabling faster time-to-route. Unlike standard compliance portals that serve as passive document repositories, CORP establishes a "Live Readiness-Gate" that continuously monitors carrier status in real time. Pilot validations have demonstrated a 28% average increase in onboarding speed and a 22% reduction in compliance errors.

How is this different from traditional compliance portals like Highway or RMIS?

+
Traditional compliance portals tell you if a carrier is "safe" but not if they are "ready" to move a specific refrigerated load from Derby to London with the correct equipment certifications. CORP unifies insurance status, service-specific capabilities, and load-execution tracking into a single technical pipeline. The RFFE identifies in real time how a compliance break (e.g. insurance expiry) is impacting usable fleet capacity a bridge that current siloed competitors have not overcome.

Which existing systems does CarrierReadiness integrate with?

+
CarrierReadiness is built as a model-agnostic infrastructure it sits above any underlying TMS or ERP including McLeod, MercuryGate, and major ELD providers. The platform connects via secure API nodes (£200 one-off setup) that capture live compliance signals from existing systems without requiring a full technology replacement. Integration is typically completed within hours, not weeks, allowing immediate carrier readiness governance.

What is "Capacity Equity" and why does preserving it matter?

+
Capacity Equity is the proprietary operational vetting logic that experienced transport coordinators carry knowing which carriers are reliable for specific lane types, how to handle documentation gaps, and how to scale fleet capacity without compliance exposure. With high staff turnover in UK logistics, this knowledge is permanently lost every time a senior coordinator departs. CarrierReadiness captures and encodes this intelligence into machine-executable "Readiness Logic," ensuring new staff reach vetting proficiency in 14 days instead of 45.

Is CarrierReadiness compliant with UK and US data regulations?

+
Yes. CarrierReadiness is architecturally designed to comply with UK GDPR, ICO guidance on AI and Data Protection, the UK's Principles-Based AI Framework (DSIT), and the October 2025 FMCSA unified USDOT identification mandate. The platform provides audit-ready forensic reporting for regulatory compliance inquiries. Secure "Readiness Nodes" with end-to-end encryption ensure sensitive carrier identification data is captured without compromising data privacy standards.

What sectors and logistics types does CarrierReadiness serve?

+
CarrierReadiness is purpose-built for UK and US independent 3PL brokerages, food distribution and cold-chain agencies, high-volume courier and last-mile houses, and founder-led transport services where the founder is the primary source of carrier vetting expertise. The platform is particularly powerful for SMEs in the East Midlands "Golden Triangle" needing structured onboarding to support rapid scale without proportional headcount growth.

How do I get started with CarrierReadiness?

Getting started is simple no code required. After selecting your plan (from £150/month), you complete a Readiness Baseline setup session (approximately 60 minutes) where you input your carrier data and compliance requirements. Secure API Nodes are configured to connect to existing TMS and ELD systems.
